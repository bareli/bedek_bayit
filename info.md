# Bedek Bayit (בדק בית)

A household maintenance tracker with an Israeli flavor. For each task it shows when it was last done and when it is next due.

- 21 Israeli preset tasks, including the AC filter, Tami4, solar heater anode, smoke detectors, gas hose, mamad filter, chimney before Pesach and mezuzot
- Schedules can repeat every N days, weeks, months or years, yearly around a Jewish holiday, yearly on a date, or follow a sensor (for example Tami4 Edge)
- Reminders come with Done and Snooze buttons on your phone, and none are sent on Shabbat or Yom Tov
- Every completion is saved with a note, the cost in ₪ and who did it
- Entities: a sensor and a button for each task, plus a to-do list and a calendar
- A Hebrew (RTL) and English card, plus a sidebar page
